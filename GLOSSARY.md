# ai-for-developers

A collection of narrative content about AI for developers, both slide-based and prose, published as a single website.

## Language

**Topic**:
A subject label, such as agentic engineering, software factory, or AI developer governance, drawn from a curated list. Every Deck and Essay carries one or more Topics.
_Avoid_: Tag, subtopic, category, section

**Deck**:
A golden, evergreen asset that tells exactly one narrative as a sequence of Slides. It is revised in place rather than republished.
_Avoid_: Presentation, slideshow, slides (for the whole), narrative (as a separate thing)

**Slide**:
A single screen within a Deck.

**Essay**:
A dated prose piece read as a single scrolling page, published once and rarely revised.
_Avoid_: Blog post, writing, musing, article
