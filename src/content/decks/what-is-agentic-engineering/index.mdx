<Slide layout="title">
  # What Is Agentic Engineering?

  Building software with AI agents in the loop.
</Slide>

<Slide>
  ## Why now?

  - Models can **plan**, not just complete
  - Agents can **use tools**: shells, tests, browsers
  - The bottleneck moves from typing to **judgement**
</Slide>

<Slide layout="statement">
  Agents are teammates, not autocomplete.
</Slide>

<Slide>
  ## Two ways to work

  <TwoColumn>
    <Fragment slot="left">
      ### Autocomplete

      You drive every keystroke. The model suggests the next line.
    </Fragment>
    <Fragment slot="right">
      ### Agentic

      You set the goal and the guardrails. The agent plans, acts, and reports back.
    </Fragment>
  </TwoColumn>
</Slide>

<Slide layout="section">
  ## Part 2: **Guardrails**
</Slide>

<Slide>
  ## What makes it work

  1. A clear spec: what "done" means
  2. Fast feedback: tests the agent can run
  3. A human review at the boundaries

  <Callout>Agents amplify your process, good or bad.</Callout>
</Slide>
